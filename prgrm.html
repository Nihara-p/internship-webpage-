Project Overview

The objective of Phase 1 is to clean and validate retail data exported from the company's ERP system. Accurate data helps reduce reporting errors and provides a reliable foundation for business analysis.

Data Cleaning Process

The main activities specified in the Phase 1 requirements.

01

Explore the Data

Review rows, columns, primary IDs and potential data errors.

02

Format as Excel Tables

Organize the data into tables for easier filtering and analysis.

03

Standardize Text

Remove unwanted spaces and hidden characters and standardize text.

04

Validate Customer Data

Check email formats, missing phone numbers, duplicate IDs and ages.

05

Validate Inventory

Flag invalid stock values and create a cleaned stock field without overwriting original values.

06

Analyze Orders

Extract month, year and quarter and examine order statuses.

07

Review Payments & Returns

Analyze payment methods, payment success and return reasons.

08

Prepare Excel Reports

Use PivotTables, charts and dashboard elements to summarize the cleaned data.
